Commit Message

[SW-102303] gaudi3: update fake fuse indication to slv mechanism

Currently, to receive fake fuse data from master, slave die polls
on master die sw status bit whether master die completed fuse init.
It was recommendation by HW team to use slave die register to indicate
slave die above fake fuse data availability. This patch updates logic to
use scratchpad register of slave die to indicate fake fuse data
is available.
