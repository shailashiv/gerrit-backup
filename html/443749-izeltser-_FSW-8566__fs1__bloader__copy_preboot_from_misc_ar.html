Commit Message

[FSW-8566] fs1: bloader: copy preboot from misc arc ccm

At the moment, when working with StandAlone Preboot, a fresh copy of
preboot is stored in the MNG.ICCM and copied to SPSRAM after every
reset by the boot loader.

Since MNG.ICCM is used for MNG application, and this application
is about to be tested on the palladium, we need an alternative
location for the preboot image.

Since MISC.ICCM is not used, we can store the image there.

In addition, additional location for large preboot app is changed
to MISC.DCCM
