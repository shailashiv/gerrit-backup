Commit Message

[SW-42722] sec: fwupd: allow fit access on resp

Need to make sure that response's fit content, should be accessible by the
controller in security mode.
The controller-device share area may be blocked as part of its behavior.
When the device send out a response with an additional content. The
share area, where the content located, should be unblocked by the
preboot, allowing access from the host. When sending out a status with
FIT, the shared area gets unblocked. And only blocked again after
receiving RST_STATE event from the controller.
