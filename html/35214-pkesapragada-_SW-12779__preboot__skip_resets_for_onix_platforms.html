Commit Message

[SW-12779] preboot: skip resets for onix platforms

FLR and HOT Reset registers are not part of PSOC block.
Accessing them on Onix platforms is causing an issue
where the onix is completely getting reset. This issue
is present with both ARM and ARC scenarios. To avoid this
behavior, skip the reset checking for onix platforms
by marking it as a weak function.
