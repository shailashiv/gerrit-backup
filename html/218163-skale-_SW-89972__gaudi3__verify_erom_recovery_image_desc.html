Commit Message

[SW-89972] gaudi3: verify erom/recovery image descriptor

EROM/Recovery image descriptors are stored at 0x0 offset. In previous
platform only hash verification was perform on erom images and executed
by ROM. Signature verification on EROM images was not perfomed as we
couldnt meet deadline of 100ms to establish PCIe link if we would
perform signature verification of three images (ppboot primary,
ppboot secondary and PCIe EROM).

As per OCP spec, any image from SPI should not be executed unverified.
Due to this requirement, this patch adds support to verify signature on
EROM image descriptor. Due to new design in gaudi3 where signature
verification is performed on image descriptor instead of individual
ppboot image and only hash verfication is performed on ppboot image,
we will be well within 100ms deadline with signature verification
enabled for EROM image descriptor.
