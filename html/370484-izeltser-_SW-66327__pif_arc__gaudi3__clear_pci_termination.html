Commit Message

[SW-66327] pif_arc: gaudi3: clear pci termination

In secure boot mode, the access from PCIe to NoC is blocked by HW FSM.
Due to this, any access from PCIe I/F to ASIC will be blocked.
FW need to clear boot termination in order to un-block the access.
According to embedded PRM 3.8.3.2.1 the termination should also be
cleared during PCIe init.
