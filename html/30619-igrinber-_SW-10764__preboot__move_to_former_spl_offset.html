Commit Message

[SW-10764] preboot: move to former spl offset

The Preboot binary was placed additionally to SPL offset at the exactly
same time the SPL was removed. Thus currently (for smoother switch) we
have Preboot binary at 2 offsets:
Goya and Gaudi: 0x1A0000 and 0xA0000
Goya2         : 0x1C0000 and 0xC0000

The second image will live for some time (for smoother switch) and
eventually will be removed as a part of reduction of the SPI image size.
Switch to use Preboot binary at the lower offset - former SPL offset.
