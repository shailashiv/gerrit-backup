[SW-33162] gaudi: sp sram wipe only on cold boot

In preparation for boot info communication between u-boot/linux and
preboot, preboot may wipe SP SRAM only on cold boot. This wipe is needed
for initializing ECC, hence it's only relevant on cold boot.
