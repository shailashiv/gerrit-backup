[SW-0] rom: remove sleep in recovery indication

As the master's recovery indication is a pulse of 10us, the slave has
a limited window to capture it. Due to this, it is better to poll for
the recovery indication continuously without sleep. As the slave does
not have a parallel execution scenario, this change would not impact
functionally.
