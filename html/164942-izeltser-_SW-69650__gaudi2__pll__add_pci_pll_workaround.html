[SW-69650] gaudi2: pll: add pci pll workaround

Due to clock domain crossing issues in Gaudi2, pci pll div0 frequency
must be equal or lower than div2 for pci phy fw upgrade.
Otherwise the phy fw upgrade is very much likely to fail and pci link
won't be established.
As a workaround, at first pci pll div0 and div2 are both being set to
1/5 pll clk (100MHz), and after pci link is up we move pci pll div0 to
his nominal frequency which is pll clk (500MHz).
