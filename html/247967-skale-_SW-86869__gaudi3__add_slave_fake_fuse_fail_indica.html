Commit Message

[SW-86869] gaudi3: add slave fake fuse fail indication

Currently, in case of fake fuse, slave die waits for master
die to complete fake fuse init and then read required values
from scratchpad register. However in case of timeout waiting
for master die to complete fake fuse init, slave die just return
and fuse values for slave die remains 0. This patch adds timeout
indication in ROM status udpate.
