Commit Message

[SW-137121] gaudi3: erom pcie: added cold flops mechanism

In erom, we bringup PCIe link up through ARC1, not through
PIF ARC. To indicate this to ppboot, added cold flops mechanism.

So that ppboot, can read the cold flops and warn the users
PIF ARC FW and PCIe PHY FW are not running, to bring it
up, perform cold boot.
