Commit Message

[SW-35427] gaudi2: image pointer offset change

Changing the SPI flash layout of Gaudi2 legacy boot flow to remove the
holes in between. Changing the image pointer flash offset to match
the new layout as below:

Sector  image
0	BTL primary
1	BTL secondary
2	BTL PRSTN1
3	BTL PRSTN2
4	BTL PCIE1
5	BTL PCIE2
6	Image pointers ARM
7	UENV (reservd for any internal testing procedure)
8	Preboot1
9	Prebbot2
