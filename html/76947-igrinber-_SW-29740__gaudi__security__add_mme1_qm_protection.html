[SW-29740] gaudi: security: add mme1_qm protection

The MME1_QM's interface clock (LBW) is disabled by default and thus to
access it, it must be enabled. Another option is to protect MME1_QM from
access and thus prevent any "non-clocked" access.
Use additional RR to cover the MME1_QM.
