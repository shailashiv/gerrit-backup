[SW-85391] greco: clear eeprom ready indication on spsram

U-boot and ARCPID poll on spsram address which indicates
that eeprom data is ready (copied there by arc mgmt), But since
arcmgmt can boot after uboot/arcpid and the spsram data is only
cleared in cold reset it can cause a scenario where arcpid
and uboot read old eeprom data.

Clearing the ready indication in preboot will prevent
the scenario above and will keep arcpid and uboot polling
for refreshed eeprom data.
