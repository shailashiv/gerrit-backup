[FSW-418] fs1: remove pcr idx get apis in ppboot

This patch removes the functions to fetch the
TPM PCR index in FS1 ppboot. In FS1, ppboot
will not be involved in extending of TPM PCRs.
SVN and manifest hash will be extended in
arcboot and fit hashes will be extended in
preboot.
