Commit Message

[SW-33611] gaudi: security: close nics macs

Recently, we have discovered an integration bug of the NICs MACs
leading to Gaudi device freeze if the MACs address space is improperly
used.
To avoid the confusion and consequences, close the MACs address space
with the RRs.
