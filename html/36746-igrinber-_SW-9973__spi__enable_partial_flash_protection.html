Commit Message

[SW-9973] spi: enable partial flash protection

As a preparation for partial (per device) SPI flash protection, we
change the condition for detecting the protection bits and SRWD bit, so
it will work with the partial flash protection variants.
Also rename the PROT_MASK into ALL_PROT_MASK, so devices can override
the PROT_MASK with device specific protection mask.
