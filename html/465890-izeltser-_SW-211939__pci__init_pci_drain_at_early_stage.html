Commit Message

[SW-211939] pci: init pci drain at early stage

Until now PCI drain was initialized after pci init, which might be too
late. it is moved to early init stage.
In addition, in gaudi3, the pci init code is accessing pcos regs from
the PIF ARC and we see that sometimes there is a drain, probably because
PLLs are not initialized yet. To solve this the PCI LBW drain timeout is
increased for gaudi3.
