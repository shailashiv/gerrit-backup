[SW-180232] pmc: persist effecters and thresholds

Persist effecter data (op_state and value) and sensor threshold data
across device resets. The data is stored in the oobi section and
will be cleared on power loss. On start, the data is read from the
oobi section and then applied to each effecter/sensor. Entries
that are not found are ignored but not removed from oobi
persistence (could be a soley preboot/runtime effecter).
