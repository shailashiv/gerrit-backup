Commit Message

[FSW-418] fs1: bootrom: configurable wait for tpm2_init

This patch adds configurable timeouts for the
tpm2_init execution.

With a short timeout, tpm2_init can be invoked
periodically, allowing other ROM tasks to continue
in parallel. Alternatively, a long timeout enables
tpm2_init to execute in a single operation.
