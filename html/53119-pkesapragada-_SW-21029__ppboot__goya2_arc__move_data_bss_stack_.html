[SW-21029] ppboot: goya2_arc: move data/bss/stack sections to dccm

In Goya2, PPBoot executes from FW SRAM that is "read-only"
for ARCs. Hence Data/BSS and Stack section updates would not
reflect right values. So move them to DCCM. Since DCCM was
also used by ARCBoot ROM for its internal stack/bss, PPboot
starts using from the second half of DCCM (lower 8KB)
