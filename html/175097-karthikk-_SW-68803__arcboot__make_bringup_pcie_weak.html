[SW-68803] arcboot: make bringup pcie weak

Due to major changes in gaudi3 (usage of image pointers to extract
image properties) compared to gaudi2, the data processing and
validation is completely different. Hence making the function
to bringup pcie images as 'weak' in preparation to make this
function project specific for gaudi3 whilst maintaining backward
compatibility for existing projects.
