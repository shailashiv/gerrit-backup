Commit Message

[SW-14252] ppboot: goya2: initialize arc0

This patch adds support to perform required initialization for
ARC0 before copying zephyr images in ICCM and DCCM.

As part of initialization, reset vector addr is set to 0x0 location of
ICCM and ARC is brought out of reset. Note that ARC remains in halt
state on out of reset.
