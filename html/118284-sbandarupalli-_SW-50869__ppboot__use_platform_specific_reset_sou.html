Commit Message

[SW-50869] ppboot: use platform specific reset sources

Gaudi1 and Greco has common bit definitions in PSOC_GLOBAL_CONF_RST_SRC
register for reset cause. But in Gaudi2 reset cause bit definitions are
modified and not backward compatible.

updated usage of reset causes in multiple functions to do pcie
initialization only for COLD and PRSTN resets. Not assuming that rst_src
less than HL_WD_RST is only COLD and PRSTN resets.
