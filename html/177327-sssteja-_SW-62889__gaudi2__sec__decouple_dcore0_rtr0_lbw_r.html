[SW-62889] gaudi2: sec: decouple dcore0_rtr0 lbw rr in pcie rr

Current logic for PCI LBW RR configures even dcore0_rtr0 as this
rtr blocks pcie inbound traffic as well. But, this rtr also blocks
other dcore engines like PCIE_VDEC, PMMU, etc as well.

So, it is decided not to configure dcore0_rtr0 for PCIE LBW RR
range as the inbound PCIE traffic is anyways controlled by PCIE
MSTR RR.

Hence, this patch removes the dcore0_rtr0 from pcie rr config.
