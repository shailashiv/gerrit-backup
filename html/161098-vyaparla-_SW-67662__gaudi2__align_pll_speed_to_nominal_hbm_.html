[SW-67662] gaudi2: align pll speed to nominal hbm 1.6

This change aligns DCORE#_IF and DCORE#_MESH PLLs to
Nominal, HBM 1.6. This change is required for the PLLs
to run at high speed inorder to speed up sha384 calculation
on boot fit itb to complete faster to avoid LKD timeout
issue in dynamic comms protocol
