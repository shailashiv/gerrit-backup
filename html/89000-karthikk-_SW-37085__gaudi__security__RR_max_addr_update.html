[SW-37085] gaudi: security: RR max addr update

Normally for LBW RRs min<hit<max is the
configuration norm followed for min and max
address ranges. The hit address is not inclusive
of min and max, hence (min-1) and (max+1)
are configured respectively as range addresses.

But for RR 'LBW_RR_DBG_ETR' the max address is
0x7F_FFFF_FFFF, for which (max+1) would not be
a valid address location. As 0x7F_FFFF_FFFF is
the last addressable location in gaudi1 the max
address will be configured as max and not (max+1).

This is special case exception for LBW RR configuration.
Due to this the last byte will not be included in the
hit range.
