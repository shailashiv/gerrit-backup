[SW-42526] ras: add asic serial number in preboot

Asic serial number support is added in preboot for both
direct (offset 11-30) and indirect (opcode 0) commands.

In direct command for compatibility reasons 11-12 is for
pcie device id and 13-30 is for asic serial number.
