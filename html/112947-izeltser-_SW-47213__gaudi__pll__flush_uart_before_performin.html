Commit Message

[SW-47213] gaudi: pll: flush uart before performing wa

During the pci pll workaround we are changing the uart clock. If the
uart is in the middle of a character transmission at this moment
corrupted data will be received on the other side.
in order to avoid that, before touching the PLLs we wait until
uart has finished the last transaction.
Since this wait state might take a little while, we don't consider
this operation as "fast" anymore.
