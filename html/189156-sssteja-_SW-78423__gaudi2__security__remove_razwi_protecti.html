[SW-78423] gaudi2: security: remove razwi protection in psoc and noc rr

This is a cherry-pick from https://gerrit.habana-labs.com/#/c/188001/
Though this is a pure preboot change, this is still ported to ppboot
to make sure the common files are aligned until we separate ppboot and
preboot common files.

As per the HBW spec, razwi section (0x1000007FF7FFF000 -
0x1000007FF7FFFFFF) must not be protected by RR. This patch fixes this
issue by configuring psoc and pcie rr to protect only till SP SRAM end
and noc rr to protect till PCIE FW SRAM RSVD. Note: RAZWI section is
above PCIE FW RSVD region which will not be protected.

Also, pcie and psoc rr are configured to protect only till SP SRAM and
opens PCIE FW SRAM due to a HW bug. To mitigate this, iATU excludes PCIE
FW SRAM region and thus this blocks PCIE initiator transactions. PSOC
initiator transactions towards this region would get block on NOC/RTR
RRs.
