Commit Message

[SW-77546] greco: mask lbw region pcie addr dec interrupt

This patch mask pcie addr dec interrupt raised due to lbw region error.
Currently its not mapped in cause register
(mmPCIE_WRAP_PCIE_IC_SEI_INTR_IND) of this interrupt and we sometimes
get this interrupt with cause 0.
After discussion with HW team , will decide how to take care of this
and then will unmask it.
