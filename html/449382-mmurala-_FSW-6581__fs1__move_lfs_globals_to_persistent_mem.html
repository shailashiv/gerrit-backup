Commit Message

[FSW-6581] fs1: move lfs globals to persistent mem

LFS is planned to use across all subsequent
modules and once boot rom handover control to
next module, lfs globals overwritten as part
of data section reused for next module.
To avoid this, we are placing all lfs globals
to persistent memory.
