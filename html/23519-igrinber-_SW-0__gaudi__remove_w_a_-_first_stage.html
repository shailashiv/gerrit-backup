[SW-0] gaudi: remove w/a - first stage

In the first stage, we remove the w/a from all PLLs, but PCI.
We still leave 2 w/a:
1) PCI PLL is still configured in BTL - output 0 and 1 are switched in
   BTL, output 2 and 3 are switched in the W/A callback in preboot.
2) Preboot location is still in scratch pad SRAM.
