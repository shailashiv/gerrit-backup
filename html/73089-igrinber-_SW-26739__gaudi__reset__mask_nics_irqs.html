Commit Message

[SW-26739] gaudi: reset: mask nics irqs

When a device is going to perform reset, we no longer interested
in receiving the interrupts from NICs. In fact they are just
interrupting the regular reset flow.
Disable the NICs interrupts the first thing in handling the reset flow.
