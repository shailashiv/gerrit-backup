Commit Message

[SW-156330] gaudi2: tpm: remove second tpm init

In gaudi2, tpm_init is called twice. During the PCR
extension for ppboot, tpm_init is called initially,
and subsequently, a second call happens during
the PCR extension of preboot. The primary purpose of
the tpm_init function is to discover the TPM device,
making the second call redundant. Therefore, removing
the second tpm_init call.
