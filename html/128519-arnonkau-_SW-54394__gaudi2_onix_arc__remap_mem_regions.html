[SW-54394] gaudi2,onix,arc: remap mem regions

On onix the memory the RAM is 6M, and split into 2.
The first 3M located on lower end of HBM0, and the other 3M located
on the upper end of HBM3.

The existing RAM's work area regions, is not suited to the limited
memory of the Onix. The FWUPD area is not usable, Use the LKD region
instead. the LKD region starts at offset 2M, which leave us with only
1M, as the memory continuation is broken after 3M.
The Region 1M-2M, is reserved for the Uboot.
