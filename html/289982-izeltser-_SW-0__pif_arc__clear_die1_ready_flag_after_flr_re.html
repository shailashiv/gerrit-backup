Commit Message

[SW-0] pif_arc: clear die1 ready flag after flr reset

Due to a HW bug described in H9-5478, the die ready indication may
remain high while die1 is being reset.
As a workaround, firmware clears the die ready indication after
triggering the reset to the other die.
