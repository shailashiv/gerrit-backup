Commit Message

[SW-22078] ppboot: goya2: bring arc out of reset

Currently in BTL based flow, ARCs are not used for any operations.
There is requirement to run BTL based flow till preboot and then load
zephyr on ARC0 and ARC1 as part of boot fit flow. This patch add changes
to perform reset vector change and bring both ARCs out of reset as part
of ppboot flow. This is required to copy fit images in ARC ICCM and DCCM
memories.
