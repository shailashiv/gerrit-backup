[SW-22029] pcie: update pcie synopsys workaround 01038891

As part of goya2 ASIC bringup, some extra register settings were
needed to be added for PCIe synopsys workaround case 01038891
to switch between gen3 and gen4 link speeds. This changes are
already made in ppboot which takes care of PCIe link bring up
and is recommended by HW team to also include in PCIe recovery
flow in arcboot ROM where synopsys workaround is already implemented.
This patch adds those additional register settings.
