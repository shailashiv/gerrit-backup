Commit Message

[SW-188948] gaudi3: tpm: reduce wait time for status

This patch reduces the TPM delays from 5 ms to 200us.
This done to avoid long waits which contribute to
overall increase in boot time of gaudi3. This change
brings down the combined time to perform TPM startup
and TPM PCR extend by 60ms.
