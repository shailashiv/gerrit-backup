Commit Message

[SW-170792] gaudi3: pif: don't turn mctp msg to inbound

As a reset default MCTP messages are turned to an inbound transactions
to the NOC.
Since at the moment those transactions are not handled and not expected,
this translation is turned off.
In addition, the MCTP Queue is getting overfilled because the MCTP msgs
are not handled. this event is masked for now.
