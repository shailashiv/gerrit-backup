[SW-21702] goya2: replace delay with register check

Currently, delay is used of 300 microseconds to wait for PHY init
and executing Synopsys phy WA. This patch replaces delay with
sram_init_done registers check to confirm PHY init followed by
executing Synopsys workaround.
