[FSW-4100] fs1: pldm: init pll dividers only

On pldm emulator only the PLL dividers are implemented, the analog part
of the PLL is not emulated and the PLL is fed with the nominal clock.
Hence need to init the analog part of the PLL and wait for lock, only
dividers should be set.
