[SW-4185] gaudi: pll: raise pci lbw clock

The PCI PLL is different from others as it supplies clocks to
PCI, PSOC, SPI, etc. and needs special care while switching it.
We switch the PCI PLL HBW and the LBW clocks in the BTL which includes
gating them, but we can't do the same for DIV2 output.
Moreover, DIV2 and DIV3 outputs do not need any W/A to be switched as
their frequency is below 400MHz.
Therefore, we do the LBW switch in BTL and just switch (no gating) of
the DIV2,3 in Preboot.
For this we use the special HL_PLL_WA_NOGT enum value, so no gating will
be done during the switch.
