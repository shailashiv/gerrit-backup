[SW-51026] gaudi: security: fix mme protect

The protection bits setting for MME ACC and SBAB were not configured
correctly due to a bug in base calculation.
base variable must be calculated as part of the MME walk over loop and
not outside it once.
Fix protection bits setting by fixing the base calculation.
