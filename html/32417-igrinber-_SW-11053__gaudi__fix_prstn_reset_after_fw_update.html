Commit Message

[SW-11053] gaudi: fix prstn reset after fw update

After firmware update a reboot of host wouldn't bring-up the link.
This is caused by cold flops not being cleared after firmware update
finished. This would cause after first PRSTn reset not to initialize
the PCIe, but clear the cold flop. Only after the second PRSTn the
PCIe link would come up.

Now we clear the cold flops after booting and not bringing up PCIe
because link was already up.
