Commit Message

[SW-50527] gaudi: security: remove tpc cfg/qm prot

The DBG TPC_CFG and DBG TPC QMAN pages are an exact mirror of the same
pages in the TPC CFG space, so we should not try to protect them twice.
This can result in exception because when we try to protect the second
time from the DBG space, we do not have the privilege level bit and try
to access the protected area.
Remove the above pages redundant protection.
