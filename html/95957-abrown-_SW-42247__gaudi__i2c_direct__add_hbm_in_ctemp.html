[SW-42247] gaudi: i2c_direct: add hbm in ctemp

CTemp, composite temperature is defined as maximum temperature
of all onchip sensors and all HBM sensors. As to HBM sensors
it is a bit more tricky: CTemp is actually an indication for
critical temperature (so that BMC can operate fans for example),
and since HBM critical temperature is lower than onchip sensors,
it need a "delta addition" first to be added to the real temperaure.
