Commit Message

[SW-173074] gaudi3: add delay for fifo pop

It is observed that after setting pop bit in
fifo control register it takes little time to
remove the entry from the fifo. This patch
adds 1 micro second delay after setting the
pop bit without this delay the entries in the
fifo does not get reduced when pop is executed.
