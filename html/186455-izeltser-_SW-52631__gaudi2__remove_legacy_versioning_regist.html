Commit Message

[SW-52631] gaudi2: remove legacy versioning registers

The versioning information of the preboot and efuse is passed
to other FW components via the bootinfo data, and to the host driver
via the COMMS protocol.
The registers that were used for the legacy method in gaudi2 are
being removed.
