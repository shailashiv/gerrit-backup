Commit Message

[SW-36011] gaudi: preserve nic en on reset

NIC enable info till now was saved in scrachpad by first
saving it in boot_info of spsram and only when loading
u-boot it was finalized into scrachpad sram.
Yet, if the fit is received more than once, or in other
scenario, we need the capability to finalize it only once
and yet assure that it is preserved it in scratchpad.
This patch takes care for both saving boot_info and for
finalize into spsram on reset and when loading image.
