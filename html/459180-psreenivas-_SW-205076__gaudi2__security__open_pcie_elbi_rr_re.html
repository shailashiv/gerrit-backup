[SW-205076] gaudi2: security: open pcie elbi rr region

The elbi rr if special glbl spare register 2 and 3 are used
for arm tx channel in hl-smi. This patch adds support to open
elbi rr region using range register and other registers in this
region are protected via protection bits.
