Commit Message

[SW-223975] u-boot: fix reset after reading flash

We didn't wait after sending wfe to device and
in some cases called reset in recovery mode before
the cpu finished to enter wfe and write the boot_info
to SRAM this caused the boot info to be corrupted.

This adds wait for wfe that to prevent potential data corruption.
