[SW-227605] g2e: support bis-e-compliant preboot

In case reading real fuse or fake fuse from flash fails, then if the card
is a G2 PCIe card (HL288), then assume it is a BIS-Compliant card, which
is a BIS-E-Compliant card. In addition, fix a minor typo in comments,
and not in a separate commit.
