[FSW-14050] fs1: palad: bloader: support comp-die

COMP-DIE and IO-DIE are basically different:
in COMP-DIE FW-MEM contains: MNG ARC and RST ARC,
while IO0-DIE contains also: MISC and PID ARCs.
Hence, bootloader for each behaves slightly different:
io-die bloader: copy preboot from misc to spsram
comp-die bloader: copy preboot from mng to spsram
