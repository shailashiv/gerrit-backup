Commit Message

Revert "[SW-166373] gaudi2: fix tpm boot failure"

This reverts commit e71e3c04514da8ff1ccad89267f8021feb41aca6.
Due to HW bug, it is decided to delay de-assertion of perst
by 20 seconds after power good. Hence waiting for prstn will
always result in failure in cold and prstn reset and pcr extend
will fail. however an additional hard reset is issued in preboot
after running BIST test post pcie link up and tpm_init should succeed
and tpm pcr extends will pass. This patch removes waiting for prstn in
tpm_init.
