Commit Message

[SW-13500] gaudi: fix instability in pci lanes

The amount of time needed to wait between phy init
and executing Synopsys phy W/A differs between HL200 and HL205,
selecting different delays stabilized the first while shook off
the last, this commit removes the delay needed and
replaces it in a monitor loop which waits for indication
from phy (ext_ld_done) which was measured to be around
15us, this changes was tested and proven to be stable
of both HL200 and HL205
