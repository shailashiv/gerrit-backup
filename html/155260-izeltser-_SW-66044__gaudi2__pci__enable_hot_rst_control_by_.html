Commit Message

[SW-66044] gaudi2: pci: enable hot rst control by fw

So far FW is managing the response to pci hot reset, but the HW
doesn't know about it, so both FW and HW are reacting to the hot
reset and collision might occur.
In order to avoid that, the relevant register is updated flagging
the HW that FW takes control of the hot reset response.
