Commit Message

[SW-50880] greco: arc: add pci pll workaround

Due to clock domain crossing issues in Greco, pci pll div0 frequency
must be equal or lower than div2 for pci phy fw upgrade.
otherwise the phy fw upgrade is very much likely to fail and pci link
won't be established. In current flow of greco, div0 and div2 are
maintained at refclock of 50MHz making sure the condition is met.

This patch add change to set PCIe/CPU PLL frequency to actual target
frequencies once pcie initialization is completed.
