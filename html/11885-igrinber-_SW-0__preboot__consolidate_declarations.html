[SW-0] preboot: consolidate declarations

There are several declarations that are used by preboot common code,
but for some reason declared (and several even implemented) in
goya/gaudi specific headers.
This restricts the headers inclusion flexibility and make the
maintenance burden bigger.

Move the declarations into the common header and move the
implementations into the .c files.
