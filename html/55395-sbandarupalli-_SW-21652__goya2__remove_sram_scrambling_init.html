[SW-21652] goya2: remove sram scrambling init

in case of legacy boot flow, preboot gets loaded into SRAM and
scrambling init can't be done in preboot. it should be moved to BTL

for arcboot rom flow, sram scrambling init can happen in preboot as
preboot is loaded into scratchpad sram
