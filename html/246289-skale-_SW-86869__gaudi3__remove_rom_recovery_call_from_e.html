Commit Message

[SW-86869] gaudi3: remove rom recovery call from erom recovery

Currently, in case of erom recovery manifest load or signature
verification failure, ROM executes ROM recovery where PCIe link,
I2C and range registers are disabled based on security_enable
fuse bit.

As part of secure code review, it is recommended to keep ROM
and EROM flows separate and in case of erom recovery failure, ROM
should halt and not enter ROM recovery flow.
