[SW-114788] gaudi2: security: reduce hbw rr address range for SRAM

With the recent decision to block reserved regions in HBW to avoid
PSOC_RAZW interrupts when a user tries to access unreserved region, full
address HBW RRs are used for protecting reserved regions above PCIE_64PB
(i.e 0x0100000000000000-0x1000007FF4000000) and above HBM (i.e
0x1002000000000000 - 0xFF00000000000000)

Due to this, we need to use short RR for SRAM and since short RR has
an address format of 0x1000_<36bits>000, we cannot protect the last 4K
sector of SRAM. Hence adjusting the max address of HBW RR configuration
used for SRAM.
