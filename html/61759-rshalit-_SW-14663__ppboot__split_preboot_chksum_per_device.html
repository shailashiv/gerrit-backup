Commit Message

[SW-14663] ppboot: split preboot chksum per device

Split verification of preboot checksum according
to device. The previous assumption that all devices
can call sha256 functions has been wrong and required
refactoring: For gaudi and legacy goya2 ppboot shall
verify preboot chksum, but for goya1 (which has tighter
limitations of timing and size), no verification is done.
For goya2 ROM, the verification is done, but not
here.
