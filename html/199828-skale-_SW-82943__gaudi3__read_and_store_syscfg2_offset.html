[SW-82943] gaudi3: read and store syscfg2 offset

In gaudi3, ROM need information fuse information stored in 58
as well 59 offset. The 59 offset which is named as syscfg2 holds
information of D2D and PLL values. This patch add change to
read and store 59 fuse offset in real and fuse emulation
scenario.
