Commit Message

[SW-20154] skip clearing the top_rom_valid entries

Goya2 cleared both the TOP_ROM_VALID registers during
JTAG disable and set both to 0xFFFFFFFF during JTAG
enable. This setting is not required for Gaudi2. Hence
move this to platform specific code.
