Commit Message

[SW-39707] gaudi: hbm: mask dword repaired lanes

On ATE stage, a defective lane between HBM device to the SoC might be
detected. In case it's possible, an HARD lane repair procedure will be
followed and the defective lane will be replaced by a redundent lane.
DC connectivity test is not aware of the new lane. Thus, it can fail
due to the defective lane. DC-connectivty test failure should result in
HBM init failure.
This patch takes care to skip the repaired lane on DC-connectivity test.

HARD lane repair on ATE is capable of repairing max of 8 DWORD lanes
and 2 AWORD lanes per channel.
