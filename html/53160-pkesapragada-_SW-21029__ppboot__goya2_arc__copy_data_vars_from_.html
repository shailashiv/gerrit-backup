[SW-21029] ppboot: goya2_arc: copy data vars from FWSRAM

In secure-boot mode of Goya2, ppboot runs from FW SRAM
that is read-only. In order to facilitate usage of
initialized global variables, they have to be relocated
from FWSRAM to DCCM so that they can be updated. Hence
this patch copies such variables before jumping to
ppboot_main
