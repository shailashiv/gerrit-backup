Commit Message

[SW-10909] goya2: ppboot: skip pcie init

This patch removes the "Skip PCIe initialization"
capability as no bit is present in Goya2 for skipping
the PCIe initialization either in DumpSel or in BOOT
STRAP register. While these bits get identified,
the PCIe Initialization will always be skipped, to
meet initial power on requirements.
