[SW-30630] ppboot: goya2: check ack signal in reset arc sequence

Currently in reset ARC sequence, halt request bit is cleared without
checking halt ACK from ARC. As per recommendation from Si team, this
patch add change to wait for ACK from ARC for halt request and then
proceed with clearing HALT request and reset ARC.

Since udelay is used for waiting for ARC response, the initialization
of ARC is moved after udelay and pcie init.
