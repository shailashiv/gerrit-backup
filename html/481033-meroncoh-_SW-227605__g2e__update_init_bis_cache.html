[SW-227605] g2e: update init bis cache

Support G2E BIS revision when initializing BIS cache.
In case we can't read the BIS revision from boot info and eFuse,
then assume it is G2E BIS revision (rev 05). Otherwise, update
it according to what's read (adding G2E BIS revision use-case).
