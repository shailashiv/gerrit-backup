[SW-47213] gaudi: pll: adding pci pll workaround

Due to clock domain crossing issues in Gaudi1, pci pll div0 frequency
must be equal or lower than div2 for pci phy fw upgrade.
otherwise the phy fw upgrade is very much likely to fail and pci link
won't be established.
On the other hand we must be able to execute BMC commands reagrdless
of the pci state, which requires a minimum of pci pll div0 frequency
of ~200Mhz.
As a workaround, at first pci pll div0 and div2 are both being set to
1/4 pll clk, and after pci link is up we move pci pll div0 to pll clk
and div2 to pll/8.
