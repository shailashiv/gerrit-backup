Commit Message

[SW-29389] gaudi: security: protect psoc etr fix

It appears we should not protect the whole ETR page, as it will be
configured by the profiler (through LKD) later during its use.
Narrow down the protection to the single register AXICTL which is
responsible for setting the ETR protection level.
