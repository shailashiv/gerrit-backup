Commit Message

[SW-52119] gaudi: prevent tpc3 pcie access

The TPC3 pre-fetcher fails to access the PCIe address space in a way
that craches the PCIe bus.
To prevent this behaviour, block the TPC3 pre-fetcher from accessing the
PCIe address space by writing the high address bytes with 0x7F.
Additionally, block the high address register with protection bits to
prevent further changes from the host.
