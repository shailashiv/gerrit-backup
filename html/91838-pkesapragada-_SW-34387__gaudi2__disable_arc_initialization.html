[SW-34387] gaudi2: disable arc initialization

Boot FIT image contains zephyr mgmt and pid images
besides the ARM uboot. Until the actual zephyr
support is made available, dummy "all zero"
binaries are created, as place holders. So,
skip booting the ARCs until valid zephyr images
are made available.
