Commit Message

[SW-117389] gaudi2: move pcie elbi enable access after init

Due to time constraint in bringing pcie link it is decided to
store ppboot hash into tpm PCR after invoking arc0 to initialize
pcie link up in ppboot and the pcie elbi access should be
enabled after storing the hash hence moving pcie elbi access
after pcie init. The ppboot tpm extended storing is added
in later patch.
