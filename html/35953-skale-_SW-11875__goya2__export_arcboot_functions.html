Hi Noam, The idea here is to perform image verification the way arcboot_rom does it. That is why we are performing verification of preboot from ppboot by using arboot export functions just the way arcboot_rom does it internally (https://gerrit.habana-labs.com/#/c/36106/6/goya2_arc/common.c). For example, we pass ARCBOOT_PRIMARY and ARCBOOT_SECONDARY as argument to arcboot_flash_image_offset function.
