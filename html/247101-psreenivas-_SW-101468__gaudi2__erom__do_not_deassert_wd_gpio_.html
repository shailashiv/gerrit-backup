Commit Message

[SW-101468] gaudi2: erom: do not deassert wd gpio in automated recovery

The CPLD will deassert GPIO2 when wd gpio(GPIO62) is deasserted
by firmware, In case of automated recovery it is agreed to deassert
the wd gpio only after successful firmware update i,e. in zephyr
agent Hence do not deassert wd gpio in automated recovery.
