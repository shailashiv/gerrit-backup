Inline Comments

include/pldm.h

  • Jason Smith (Line 94): Entity aux name structs are 2 bytes larger than the regular aux name struct; That comes out to 112 bytes wasted in preboot, and 208 in mgmt. When we first wrote libpldm, we were on the fence about whether to use the union for pldm_pdr_record, or whether to break out each PDR type individually. At the time we leaned towards ease of coding and speed of getting things working. Over time the space constraints forced us to break out individual PDR types.
