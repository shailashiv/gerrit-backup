[SW-82717] rom: configure rr in recovery flow

Lbw and hbw ranges are required to be accessible over pcie. But by
default, HW blocks all ranges for read/write accesses over pcie.
Hence the selective ranges in config space are opened for lbw.

SRAM in opened in HBW in order to load fit image received over pcie.
