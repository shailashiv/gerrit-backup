Commit Message

[SW-8229] reset psoc and arc on warm reset

According to new flow in spec we change default behaviour
of reset caused by PERST, we'll reset ARC and PSOC as long
as we dont have a functioning arcboot in RAM. This will
make sure we can allways deal with PERST signal.
