[SW-20810] i2c: fit: load firmware

Use NVME indirect request port to load firmware in FIT format via I2C.

The firmware is expected to be received in 65535 chunks (which is the
maximum transmission unit for a single request). The chunks are
accumulated together into the FIT base buffer and once verified,
the FIT is loaded.
