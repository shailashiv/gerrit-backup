[SW-222861] g3: pldm: mask subsensors by platform

Some state sensors have sub-sensors that should be supported on
HL325 and not HL338. Mask select sub-sensors of the following:
	Gaudi3 power and performance state
	Gaudi3 OAM last power down cause 1
	Gaudi3 OAM last power down cause 4
	Gaudi3 OAM last power down cause 5
	Gaudi3 OAM last power down cause 7
