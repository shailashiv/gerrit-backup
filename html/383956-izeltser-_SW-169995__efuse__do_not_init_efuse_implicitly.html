Commit Message

[SW-169995] efuse: do not init efuse implicitly

efuse is initialized explicitly from the main function.
this patch removes the implicit initialization of the efuse from the
hl_fuse_read() because in complex scenarioas such as in multi-die
devices this might create undesired recursion.
