Commit Message

[SW-94323] gaudi2: watch dog de-assertion in erom recovery.

At PRSTn CPLD asserts GPIO62 and starts WD timer.
Since de-assertion of this gpio is not done in erom recovery
even after successful firmware, ROM doesn't load and verify new
firmware from SPI as it enters to recovery due to GPIO2 assertion.
Hence de-asserting this gpio in erom recovery and after successful
firmware update the ROM should load, verify and execute new firmware
from spi flash.
