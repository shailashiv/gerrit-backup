Commit Message

[SW-222861] g3: pldm: mask pdrs by platform

Some PDRs should be supported on HL325 and not HL338.
Mask the following:
	Gaudi3 OAM power sensor: ADC 48V input
	Gaudi3 OAM voltage sensor: 48V OAM Input ADC
	Gaudi3 OAM voltage sensor: B2B PVREF
	Gaudi3 current power consumption, 54V domain
	Gaudi3 Highest consumed power, 54V domain
	Gaudi3 power effecter: Limit maximal supported power envelope
	Gaudi3 energy consumed by the 54V PSU
	Gaudi3 Peak power consumption observed in 1 minute time window
	Gaudi3 Last power reduction duration
