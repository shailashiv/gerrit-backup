[SW-65171] gaudi2: pci: update preset vector at gen3 and 4

In order to avoid TO at phase2, preset vector of Gen3 and Gen4 are
being updated.

Updated preset vector of PCIe Phy to support preset 4,5,6 and 9.

the following sequence should be executed before phy reset
de-assertion:

GEN3_RELATED_OFF = 0x00402001
GEN3_EQ_CONTROL_OFF = 0x05027071
GEN3_RELATED_OFF = 0x01402001
GEN3_EQ_CONTROL_OFF = 0x05027071

Instructed by Haim Revivo.
