Commit Message

[SW-1886] pll: reduce Fvco to 1200MHz

We observe a Synchronous Aborts in about 0.5-2% of the reboots.
In our tests this has been pinned down to a PLL problem.
Statistically it was found that reducing the Fvco from 2400 MHz to
1200MHz (regardless the theory) improves the situation dramatically.
While investigation continues, reduce the Fvco of the CPU PLL to
1200MHz.
